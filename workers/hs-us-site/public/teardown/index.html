          What it is

          A reading, not a verdict

          Most quotes are hard to compare because the lines are not the same kind of thing. A teardown sorts them: labor, materials, permit, overhead, disposal and other. Then it marks what cannot be checked by anyone, because the wording gives no quantity or unit, and turns each gap into a question you can send.

          What a teardown does
          • Reads the lines and the total
          • Sorts every line by kind
          • Marks lump-sum and allowance lines
          • Notes missing crew size, hours or squares
          • Adds the public wage when you give a ZIP
          What it is not
          • A fairness verdict on the price
          • An inspection of your home
          • A bid or a counter-offer
          • A review of your contractor
          • A promise that you will save money
          How it works

          Three steps, under a minute

          1. Upload the quote

            A photo, a PDF of up to three pages, or the lines pasted as text. Add a ZIP code if you want the local wage.

          2. An AI model reads the lines

            It copies each line, its amount and its kind. Everything you see after that is computed from those lines and from public wage data, with the source printed.

          3. You get the questions

            Send them to the contractor. A contractor with a reason for each number can answer quickly.

          Warning sign

          Price good only today?

          A deadline of tonight is a sales tactic, not a cost. When a sale of $25 or more is made at your home, the FTC Cooling-Off Rule gives you until midnight of the third business day to cancel. A deadline of tonight does not shorten that.

          Ask for the quote in writing and read every line before you sign. A teardown takes seconds and needs no account.

          Questions

          Before you upload

          Is the Quote Teardown free?

          Yes. No account, no email and no payment. To keep it free for everyone, one network can run three teardowns an hour.

          What do you keep?

          An anonymous card for 24 hours: the trade, the state, the total, how the lines split by kind, and our own flags and questions. We do not keep your files or the text of the lines, and we do not train AI on your uploads. An AI model reads the pages; everything else is computed from those lines and public data. If you post the card to the board, it is reviewed before it appears. Otherwise it is deleted after 24 hours.

          Is this a verdict on my contractor?

          No. A teardown reads the lines and asks what is missing. It is not a fairness verdict, an inspection or a bid. A flag is a question to ask, not proof that anything is wrong, and a quote with no flags is not proof that it is fair.

          Where does the wage number come from?

          From the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics: the hourly wage for the trade's main occupation in your metro area, or in your state when the metro has no figure, at the mean and at the 90th percentile. In Texas we also apply the labor loading TxDOT publishes for labor markup, insurance and payroll taxes; elsewhere the wage is shown without a loading and the teardown says so. When the quote states crew size and days or hours, we multiply to give a labor-dollar range. The source and period are printed with the number.
