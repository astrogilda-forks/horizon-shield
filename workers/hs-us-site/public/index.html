How it works

Three steps. No sales call.

  1. Upload the quote and your ZIP code

    A PDF, a photo or pasted text. You can black out your name and address first. We strip location data from photos.

  2. We read every line and find the public data

    Local wage statistics, import and distribution-chain prices, the markups state DOTs pay contractors, and permit records where your city publishes them. Each reference carries its source and its formula.

  3. You get a ledger and the questions to ask

    Lines above the reference, lines within it, and lines with no public benchmark, all stated. Then the specific questions that make a contractor explain the difference.

Independence

Homeowners pay us. Contractors don't.

Many free home-cost sites earn their money from contractors, who pay for each homeowner lead. Published lead prices run from about $15 to $150 each, depending on the trade and whether the lead is exclusive (Fixr, LeadTruffle).

We take no referral fees, no lead fees, no listing fees and no commissions from contractors. Our only job is to read the quote the way you would if you had the data.

FAQ

Questions.

Is this a verdict that my quote is fair or unfair?

No. It is a price benchmark. We compare each line with public data and show how far apart they are, with the source of every number. Public-works figures and wage statistics are not residential quotes, and a contractor can have good reasons to differ. The report tells you where to ask.

Which trades and places do you cover?

Wage statistics cover every state and most metro areas. Price indexes are national. Import and distribution-chain estimates cover common building materials. Permit valuation records cover the cities that publish them, currently Austin, Boston, New Orleans, New York City, San Diego, San Francisco and Seattle. When we have no public data for a line, the report says so.

What happens to my file?

We use it only to prepare your report. We strip location data from photos, delete uploaded files within 30 days of upload, keep your report for 12 months so you can download it again, and do not use your uploads to train AI models. We do not sell or share your personal information. See the Privacy Policy.

Do you visit or inspect my home?

No. We work only from the quote, measurements, photos and description you send. We do not inspect property or judge its condition. For that, hire a licensed home inspector in your state.

Can you estimate a job before I ask for quotes?

Yes. A Detailed Estimate turns your measurements, photos and description into line items, each with a public reference range, and gives you a scope list to send contractors so their quotes line up. It is a cost reference, not a bid, and it is only as complete as the information you give us.

Do you work with contractors or get paid by them?

No. We take no referral, lead, listing or success fees, and we do not recommend contractors. Homeowners are our only customers.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

No. We do not prepare, submit or negotiate insurance claims. In many states that work requires a public adjuster license. Our report is price information only.

Is AI involved?

Yes. AI reads the quote and matches each line to public data. The numbers themselves come from the listed sources and fixed formulas, and every report carries a receipt so the calculation can be checked.
