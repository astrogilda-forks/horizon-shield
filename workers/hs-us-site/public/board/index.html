On a card

Numbers and questions, no people

Each card is written from the teardown, not copied from the quote. Wording that quoted a line is replaced, and questions that named a line are left out.

What a card shows
  • Trade and state
  • Total and number of lines
  • Share of the total by kind of line
  • Flags: what was missing or unclear
  • Questions for the contractor
What a card never shows
  • Names of the homeowner or the contractor
  • Street address or ZIP code
  • The text of the quote lines
  • The uploaded photo or PDF
  • Any verdict that the price is fair or unfair
